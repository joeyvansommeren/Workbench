# TES-269 — Needs you queue

Branch: `tes-269-needs-you-queue`. Base: `954eeed8`.

This change extends the existing queue. It keeps retained items in place and appends new items. Each row shows its reason. The filter shows the queue count, and selected rows remain visible. Arrow keys move through the queue. Command-J reveals the queue from any destination, opens the next item, and requests a VoiceOver announcement of its title and reason. Each window keeps its own filter and cursor.

Permissions and explicit questions take priority over failed checks and recorded results. Failed checks open the session's GitHub panel. A recorded result opens its review view, including for sessions attached to terminal workspaces. Quiet sessions and raw activity stay out. Accepted results return only when their recorded definition is revised; a later activity timestamp alone is insufficient. Closed or unrelated pull requests do not supply queue failures. Earlier failures keep their position while refreshing, with a refreshing label. They remain labelled as unavailable when observation fails.
